## Formula & Milk

### Kendamil Goat Infant Formula (Stage 1)
- **Brand:** Kendamil · **Type:** Goat milk-based infant formula
- **Per oz (30ml):** ~20 cal · ~0.2mg iron · ~4mg DHA · ~6.5mg choline · ~2.5µg iodine · ~13mg calcium · ~0.4µg vitamin D · ~0.5g protein · ~0.15mg zinc · ~22µg vitamin A · ~4µg folate
- **Per 240ml (8oz):** ~160 cal · ~1.6mg iron · ~32mg DHA · ~52mg choline · ~20µg iodine · ~104mg calcium · ~3.2µg vitamin D · ~4g protein
- **Status:** Fully phased out — 0oz as of July 6, 2026 (28oz → 0oz over 113 days starting Mar 16)

### Horizon Organic Growing Years Whole Milk
- **Brand:** Horizon Organic · **Type:** Whole milk with added DHA (for 1+ years)
- **Per oz (30ml):** ~19 cal · ~0mg iron · ~4mg DHA (added) · ~5mg choline · ~7µg iodine · ~38mg calcium · ~0.3µg vitamin D · ~1g protein
- **Per 240ml (8oz):** ~150 cal · ~0mg iron · ~32mg DHA · ~40mg choline · ~56µg iodine · ~300mg calcium · ~2.5µg vitamin D · ~8g protein
- **Key difference from formula:** Zero iron, but ~3× more iodine, ~3× more calcium, ~2× more protein
- **Daily target:** 16–20oz. AAP recommends max 16–24oz/day for toddlers. At 20oz: calcium ~760mg (109%), iodine ~140µg (156%). Cereal mixing milk (~3oz) adds further.

### Vitamin D Drops
- **Dose:** 400 IU (10µg) daily — auto-add to every daily analysis
- **Started:** May 31, 2026
- **Why:** Neither formula nor milk covers the 15µg (600 IU) 12-month target. Drops are the only solution.
- **With drops:** ~16–17µg vitamin D total (107–115% of target)

## Iron Cereal

### Iron-Fortified Baby Cereal (brand not specified — likely Gerber or similar)
- **Form:** Dry powder, mixed with Horizon milk (~3oz per 3T cereal)
- **Per tbsp (dry):** ~15 cal · ~2.2mg iron
- **Standard dose:** 2 tbsp dry (4.4mg iron) mixed with 2oz milk
- **Target dose:** 3 tbsp dry (6.6mg iron) mixed with 3oz milk — 94% of 7mg daily target from one food
- **Mixing liquid:** Horizon milk (transitioned from formula May 15). The ~3oz mixing milk also boosts daily calcium.
- **This is Lucas's #1 iron source.** 3T cereal days: iron avg ~141–167%. No-cereal days: iron avg ~47%. No food combination can replace it.

## Recipes

### Congee (Rice Porridge)
- **Base:** Rice cooked in water until very soft/porridge consistency
- **Nutrition per tbsp:** ~8 cal · ~0.1mg iron
- **Commonly mixed with:** salmon, cod, black cod, broccoli, carrots, cauliflower

### Salmon Congee
- **Recipe:** Congee base + flaked salmon + vegetables (typically broccoli or bok choy)
- **Salmon per tbsp:** ~15 cal · ~130mg DHA · ~0.1mg iron · ~3µg iodine
- **Typical serving:** 2–3 tbsp congee mix with ~1 tbsp salmon in it

### Cod & Broccoli Congee (White Fish Congee)
- **Recipe:** Congee base + Costco cod fish + steamed broccoli
- **Cod source:** Costco (frozen cod fillets)
- **Cod per tbsp:** ~12 cal · ~50µg iodine (5× more than salmon!) · ~35mg DHA · ~0.1mg iron
- **Typical serving:** 2–5 tbsp congee with ~0.7–1.5T cod mixed in
- **When listed as "white fish congee/porridge":** assume regular cod unless specified as black cod

### Red Lentil Dal (Homemade)
- **Recipe:** Red lentils + chicken broth + onion + olive oil + garlic + turmeric + garam masala + cumin + salt & pepper
- **Per tbsp:** ~22 cal · ~0.3mg iron (non-haem) · ~1.1g protein · ~0.6g fiber · ~15µg folate · ~0.6g fat · ~45mg sodium
- **Per 3T serving:** ~66 cal · ~0.9mg iron · ~3.3g protein · ~1.8g fiber · ~45µg folate (30% of daily target)
- **Best folate source in Lucas's diet.** Red lentils are one of the richest folate foods available.
- **Iron note:** Non-haem iron — pair with broccoli or cauliflower (vitamin C) for 2–3× better absorption
- **Sodium note:** Chicken broth + salt contribute ~45mg sodium/tbsp. Use low-sodium broth to reduce by ~50%.
- **Spice bonus:** Cumin adds ~1.4mg iron/tsp; turmeric is anti-inflammatory (curcumin)
- **Texture:** Red lentils break down completely — naturally smooth, ideal for toddlers
- **Best pairings:** + broccoli (vit C for iron absorption), + sweet potato (vit A), + congee, or spread on sourdough pancakes

### Beef Shepherd's Pie (Homemade)
- **Recipe:** Ground beef + mashed potatoes + carrots + onions
- **Per tbsp:** ~19 cal · ~0.25mg iron (haem from beef) · ~1g protein · vitamin A from carrots
- **Per 3T serving:** ~56 cal · ~0.75mg iron · ~3g protein
- **Strongest iron dinner option** — haem iron from beef is absorbed 2–3× better than plant iron
- **Pairs well with:** iron cereal (3T cereal + 3T shepherd's pie = ~7.5mg iron in one meal = 107% of daily target)

### Beef & Sweet Potato + Broccoli
- **Recipe:** Ground beef + mashed sweet potato + steamed broccoli
- **Per 4 tbsp serving (1.5 beef + 1.5 SW + 1 broccoli):** ~65 cal · ~2.8mg iron (haem) · ~200µg vitamin A
- **Alternative to shepherd's pie** with added vitamin A from sweet potato

### Mini Banana Muffins (Homemade)
- **Recipe:** Almond flour + banana + egg, mixed and baked. Zero added sugar.
- **Size:** 1 mini muffin ≈ 1.25 tbsp
- **Per mini muffin (1.25T):** ~31 cal · ~0.4mg iron (almond flour) · ~15mg choline (egg in batter) · ~1.5g protein · healthy fats · vitamin E
- **Same base as birthday cake** but in muffin form with added egg
- **Iron note:** Almond flour provides meaningful iron (~0.3mg/tbsp). 3 muffins = ~1.1mg iron.
- **Choline note:** Egg in the batter contributes ~15mg choline per muffin — a bonus on top of standalone eggs
- **Great breakfast food** — calorie-dense, easy to hold, pairs perfectly with scrambled egg + avocado

### Egg Muffins (Homemade, Batch-Cookable)
- **Recipe:** 6 eggs + splash of milk + ½ cup cottage cheese + 1 cup broccoli + ½ cup chicken, baked. Makes 24.
- **Size:** ~1.5 tbsp per muffin
- **Per muffin (1.5T):** ~29 cal · ~0.3mg iron · ~38mg choline · ~8µg iodine · ~2.8g protein · ~5.5mg DHA · ~3.4mg vit C
- **Per 2 muffins (~3T):** ~58 cal · ~0.6mg iron · ~75mg choline · ~16µg iodine · ~5.5g protein
- **Per 3 muffins (~4.5T):** ~87 cal · ~0.9mg iron · ~113mg choline (57% of target) · ~24µg iodine · ~8.3g protein
- **Key benefit:** Choline backup for days without standalone egg. 3 muffins ≈ 77% of a whole egg's choline but with MORE protein (8.3g vs 6g).
- **5 key foods in one bite:** egg (choline) + cottage cheese (iodine) + broccoli (vit C for iron absorption) + chicken (haem iron) + milk (calcium)
- **Built-in iron absorption:** Broccoli's vitamin C enhances iron absorption from eggs and chicken within the same food
- **Convenience:** Batch-cook 24 at once, freeze, reheat as needed. Solves the inconsistent egg habit problem.

### Birthday Cake (Lucas's 1st birthday)
- **Recipe:** Almond flour + banana cake, zero added sugar
- **Per tbsp:** ~25 cal · ~0.3mg iron (from almond flour) · protein · healthy fats · vitamin E
- **Same nutrition as mini banana muffins** but without egg in batter

### Sourdough Discard Pancakes
- **Recipe:** Sourdough starter discard + flour + butter/oil (may include egg)
- **Per tbsp:** ~33 cal · ~0.2mg iron
- **Per pancake (~2T):** ~67 cal · ~0.5mg iron
- **Calorie-dense breakfast food.** Sourdough fermentation may improve mineral absorption and gut health.
- **Best pairing:** Serve alongside scrambled egg + avocado, or spread with dal or nut butter

## Key Findings from 40+ Days of Tracking (Mar 16, 2026 – Aug 9, 2026)

1. **Iron cereal is irreplaceable.** 3T cereal days: iron avg ~150% of target. No-cereal days: ~26–74%. No food combination — even 26T of food with 10+ items, multiple meats, spinach, and enriched pita — can reach the 7mg target without cereal. Best no-cereal day ever was Aug 9 at 74% (thanks to enriched pita + spinach + lamb).
2. **Formula era ended July 6, 2026.** 28oz → 0oz over 113 days. Iron baseline from liquid is now 0mg, making cereal the ONLY concentrated iron source.
3. **Cereal + egg/egg muffins = everything works.** Every day with both exceeded iron AND choline targets. Average: ~11mg iron (157%), ~280mg choline (140%).
4. **Egg muffins solve the egg consistency problem.** Debuted Jul 6. 2 muffins lifted choline from 51% → 89%. 3 muffins → ~108% (target met). Batch-cook 24, freeze, grab as needed.
5. **Black cod is the DHA king** (~150mg/tbsp, 4× regular cod). But 3T salmon on Jul 6 set the all-time DHA record: ~481mg (385%).
6. **Vitamin D drops close the structural gap.** With 400 IU drops: 107–115%. Without: 41–56%. No food alternative.
7. **Pita bread is a surprise secondary iron source** (~0.34mg/T from enriched flour). 5T pita = ~1.7mg iron. Not a cereal replacement, but a useful contributor.
8. **Almond flour foods (banana muffins, birthday cake) provide meaningful iron** (~0.3–0.4mg/tbsp). 3 muffins = ~1.1mg iron + ~45mg choline.
9. **"Perfect days" (all targets met):** Jun 5 and Jun 10. Template: egg+avocado+muffins breakfast → fish+veggies lunch → 3T cereal+shepherd's pie dinner → drops + 16–20oz milk.
10. **Calorie target (800–1,000 cal/day):** Hit multiple times. Best: Jun 10 (~986 cal), Aug 9 (~915 cal). Calorie-dense foods: pita (~36 cal/T), avocado (~25 cal/T), almond muffins (~25 cal/T), pancakes (~33 cal/T).
11. **Food volume record:** ~26T on Aug 9. Lucas eats 3 full meals + snacks — a real toddler eating pattern.
12. **30+ different foods tracked** across the period, including Mediterranean meals (pita, lamb, Greek potatoes), multiple fish species, 4+ legumes, and 10+ vegetables.
