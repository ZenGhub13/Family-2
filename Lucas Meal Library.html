Every DRI target is met or exceeded except iodine (62–72% on these two days). To close the gap: serve cod instead of salmon 1×/week (~30 µg/oz), add nori strips to congee, and slightly increase yogurt portions. At 12 months the iodine target drops to 90 µg, making it easier.
